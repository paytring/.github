# [Paytring](https://paytring.com?utm_source=github) | Seamless Fintech Inovations

Paytring is a universal, feature-rich payment orchestration platform designed for online businesses and payment institutions. Founded in 2023 and based in Gurugram, India, our mission is to make payment integration effortless, cost-effective, and optimized for better conversions. We are a seed company co-founded by Debal Chakraborty and Keshav Munjal.
