# [Paytring](https://paytring.com?utm_source=github) | Seamless Fintech Inovations

## About Us

Paytring is a universal, feature-rich payment orchestration platform designed for online businesses and payment institutions. Founded in 2023 and based in Gurugram, India, our mission is to make payment integration effortless, cost-effective, and optimized for better conversions. We are a seed company co-founded by Debal Chakraborty and Keshav Munjal.

## What We Do

Paytring simplifies payment processes and integrations by unifying payment methods and aggregators onto a single platform. Our platform integrates with numerous payment providers and acquirers from around the world, providing a unified interface for communication, control, and management. This allows businesses to manage cash flows across multiple providers, reduce costs, and increase conversion rates.

Our platform is designed to help businesses achieve faster, more secure transactions, improve payment success rates, and gain enhanced control over payment-related data and analytics. We support all types of payments, including one-time purchases and recurring subscriptions, and facilitate cross-border transactions with real-time currency conversion.

## Key Features

*   **Unified Integration**: A single integration for all your payment gateways and acquirers.
*   **AI-Powered Intelligent Routing**: Our smart routing helps to lower payment failure rates, leading to better conversions.
*   **Real-Time Management**: Manage all payment and payout processes in real-time with a single dashboard.
*   **Secure Infrastructure**: Our reliable and secure distributed payment infrastructure is shielded from all types and sizes of DDoS attacks.
*   **Global Reach**: We simplify cross-border transactions, helping you to expand your business internationally.
